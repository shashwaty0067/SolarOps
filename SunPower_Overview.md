# ☀️ SolarOps – Solar Installation Management System

**A Smart Digital Platform for Managing End-to-End Rooftop Solar Installations**

## 📌 About the Project

SolarOps is a Solar Installation Management System designed for **SunPower Solutions** to digitize and streamline the complete rooftop solar installation process.

Currently, many solar installation businesses rely on manual processes such as WhatsApp, phone calls, spreadsheets, and paper records. These methods can lead to communication gaps, delays, data inconsistency, and difficulty tracking projects.

SolarOps aims to provide a centralized platform to manage the entire customer journey, from initial enquiry to installation and post-installation warranty support.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Digitize solar installation business operations.
- Improve customer and enquiry management.
- Simplify site surveys and quotation generation.
- Track orders, payments, and inventory.
- Monitor installation progress.
- Manage equipment serial numbers and warranties.
- Streamline customer complaints and service requests.
- Provide business insights through dashboards and reports.

## 🚀 Key Modules

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer Management | Manage customer details, enquiries, and follow-ups. |
| Site Survey | Record roof measurements, shadow-free area, and survey approvals. |
| Quotation Management | Generate quotations using predefined solar packages. |
| Order Management | Manage order confirmation and payment tracking. |
| Inventory Management | Track stock and generate material requirements. |
| Serial Number Tracking | Map equipment serial numbers to customers. |
| Installation Management | Schedule technicians and monitor installation progress. |
| Warranty Management | Track warranty activation and expiry dates. |
| Service Management | Manage complaints, repair tickets, and service requests. |
| Finance Management | Track revenue, expenses, payments, and profits. |
| Dashboard & Analytics | Monitor daily, weekly, and monthly performance. |
| Subsidy Management | Track government subsidy documentation and status. |

## 👥 User Roles

The system is intended to support different business roles, such as:

- **Admin:** Manage users, operations, and overall business activities.
- **Sales Team:** Handle customer enquiries, follow-ups, and quotations.
- **Survey Team:** Conduct and update site surveys.
- **Inventory Manager:** Manage stock and equipment.
- **Installation Team:** Update installation schedules and progress.
- **Service Team:** Handle complaints and warranty-related requests.
